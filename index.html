LLC Registration

Limited Liability Company (LLC) is described in the profile as the most common form of company registration in Qatar, with the ownership structure stated there as 51% Qatari and 49% expatriate investor.

QFC 100% Incorporation

Qatar Financial Centre (QFC) incorporation is presented as an option that allows 100% foreign ownership with a 10% corporate tax.

100% Foreign Investment

The profile lists specific activities where 100% foreign ownership may be available, including IT, health, tourism, manufacturing, agriculture and education.

Foreign Company Branch

Branch registration is described as useful when a business needs a presence in Qatar for national or international operational and logistical management.

Representative Office

Representative office registration is presented as a way for a foreign company to establish its presence in Qatar, with a shorter registration timeframe noted in the profile.
